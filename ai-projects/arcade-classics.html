Recreating Arcade Classics

AI Project

Overview

A collection of 8 iconic arcade games rebuilt from scratch over a matter of 8 days. Each game is a recreation of the spirit of the originals, playable directly in the browser.

I made these games to preserve the classics so that my kids could play them any time they want.

Tetris
Breakout
Snake
Pong
Asteroids
Space Invaders
Defender
Ms. Pac-Man

AI was used for the code but the art/music/sfx and tuning/game feel was done by humans.
